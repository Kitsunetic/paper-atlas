# ECCV 2026 Main Papers — Korean first-author affiliation audit

## Inclusion rule

Include an ECCV 2026 **main-conference** paper when its first-listed author has
at least one affiliation physically located in the Republic of Korea.  This is
an affiliation criterion, not a nationality criterion.  Joint-first authors
are not separately promoted in the current table: the first author as printed
by the publisher is used.

Korean institutions are determined from the affiliation address, including
Korean universities, government research institutes, hospitals, and domestic
company R&D sites.  A Korean company name alone is insufficient: overseas
sites such as NAVER LABS Europe and Samsung Research London are excluded.

## Coverage and status

- **75 verified or strongly source-supported papers are listed below.**
- 73 come from the currently indexed Springer ECCV 2026 chapter pages, whose
  author-affiliation metadata names a Korean location.
- Two additional ECVA main-conference entries are included from their official
  paper pages plus author/project pages: *FeDepth* (UNIST) and *Disentangling
  Rotation and Translation…* (GIST).
- This is **not yet a defensible final all-paper total**: ECVA currently lists
  2,834 distinct main-conference virtual-paper records, while the available
  Springer chapter metadata covered 919.  The remaining 1,915 ECVA records
  require an additional PDF/author-affiliation pass before “all” can be
  claimed.

## Excluded false positives

The following first authors had affiliations at foreign offices of Korean
companies, so they do not satisfy the Korea-location rule: *Multi-HMR 2*,
*Vulnerability of Privacy-Preserving Visual Localization Methods Against
Diffusion-Based Attacks*, and *Task Alignment* (NAVER LABS Europe, France);
*Geometrically Consistent Multi-view Scene Generation from Freehand Sketches*
(Samsung Research, London); and *Raw-JPEG Adapter* (Samsung AI Center,
Toronto).
