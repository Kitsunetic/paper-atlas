# ECCV 2026 main papers — first author with a Korean affiliation

## Result

**277 papers** meet this rule: the first-listed author has an affiliation physically located in the Republic of Korea.

## Complete-scope method

- The source is ECVA's official ECCV 2026 programme JSON.
- The audit covers all **2,834 Poster** records. Each has a distinct paper UID; the 28 Oral and 135 Spotlight schedule entries repeat papers already represented by these records and are not counted again.
- The inclusion test uses authors[0].institution, i.e. the first author in the programme's author order. It is an affiliation-location test, not a Korean-nationality test.
- Included institution types: Korean universities, national institutes, hospitals, and Korea-based company R&D organisations.
- Company affiliations were checked for location where the name alone was ambiguous. For example, Samsung System LSI/Hwaseong and Samsung Electronics are included; Samsung Research America, Samsung AI Center Cambridge, Samsung R&D Bangladesh, Samsung/University of Surrey, and NAVER LABS Europe are excluded.
- First-author institution strings were normalized only for counting/review, but this table preserves the wording supplied by ECVA.
