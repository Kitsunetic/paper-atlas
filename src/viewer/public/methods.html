What the atlas measures

Source boundary

The viewer is generated from the immutable official ECVA accepted-paper and virtual-program snapshots, then normalized offline against a pinned ROR release and evidence-backed alias ledgers. The local raw snapshots are deliberately not published with this static viewer.

Country semantics

A country is the verified country of the canonical organization. It is not author nationality. For a generic company affiliation, organization country may be known while the author's specific research-site country remains unclaimed. Records with no attributable organization country stay visible as coverage gaps instead of receiving a guess.

Counting modes

Full count credits each country represented on a paper once. Fractional count divides one paper equally across the distinct attributable organization countries on that paper. Both modes use all authors; future venue datasets may add independently labelled first-author or corresponding-author measures.

Graph scope

The overview graph shows country pairs co-occurring on papers. A country or institution selection opens a bounded author subgraph: it preserves the selected paper scope but limits rendered authors to the most frequent participants to remain readable. The accompanying rank and scope statistics retain complete counts.
